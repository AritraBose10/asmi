The School

ASMi Business School is affiliated with Techno India University, West Bengal, and offers AICTE-approved business programmes from its campus at Godrej Genesis, Sector V, Kolkata. "ASMI" is Sanskrit for "I am" — self-belief, identity and individual potential.

Yes. Programmes are AICTE-approved and ASMi is part of Techno India University, a recognised and accredited institution. Degrees are awarded by the university.

IBM powers analytics and emerging-technology content across the MBA and BBA. The wider Techno India Group is also one of Google's largest education partners in India, opening Google Cloud skilling pathways.

Programmes

A two-year full-time MBA with six specializations, a four-year BBA in Business Analytics, an Executive MBA for working professionals, and three-month certification courses in Marketing 4.0, HR Excellence and Entrepreneurship 4.0.

A blend of theory and practice: lectures, case studies, group discussions, seminars, workshops and direct industry interaction. Faculty are practitioners as well as academics.

At the end of the first year, after two semesters of common core across marketing, finance, operations and people.

No. Statistics and data modules start from first principles in the BBA — comfort reasoning through numbers matters more than prior coursework.

Admissions

Apply online through the application form. The process has six steps: application, document upload, authentication, counsellor interview, provisional offer letter, and programme start.

MBA: a bachelor's degree in any discipline. BBA: 10+2 from a recognised board in any stream. Executive MBA: substantial professional experience — the counsellor interview assesses fit.

Yes. ASMi welcomes international applicants; the process may carry additional documentation requirements. Write to info@asmibschool.com before applying.

Speak to the admissions office about current scholarship and fee assistance options for your programme and cycle — these vary by intake.

Placements & Careers

Yes. A dedicated placement cell handles corporate relations, profile building, interview preparation and recruiter engagement, starting in the first semester.

For the 2023–25 batch: ₹20.38 LPA highest package, ₹14.20 LPA average and ₹13.50 LPA median. Recruiters include EY, HDFC Bank, HSBC, E-Intellisense and Lifestyle International.

Yes — a summer internship sits in the MBA third semester and the BBA third year, alongside live industry projects each year.

Campus & Student Life

A vertical campus on the 15th floor of Godrej Genesis in Sector V, with modern classrooms, a business library, an analytics lab, case rooms and student lounge — in the middle of Kolkata's technology district.

Eight student-led clubs cover finance, marketing, analytics, HR, entrepreneurship, social impact, culture and sport, alongside a year-round events calendar.

An annual immersion in Malaysia with INTI International University: industry sessions, factory visits including Royal Selangor and Barleys Chocolate, and cultural fieldwork.
