Why this MBA

Built for the first ten years
of your career, not the first job

Every element of the programme is designed around one question: what will make you useful in a room full of decision-makers?

IBM-powered curriculum

Analytics, cloud and emerging-technology modules co-designed with IBM, plus Google Cloud skilling pathways through the wider Techno India Group partnership.

Learning by doing

Live projects, internships and case work run alongside every theory module. You present to practitioners, not just to professors.

12 industry mentors

Practising CXOs and founders — from HDFC Bank and HSBC to Shark Tank India alumni — mentor students through the two years.

International study tour

A ten-day immersion in Malaysia with INTI International University: industry sessions, factory floors and cultural fieldwork.

Placement from day one

The placement cell starts interview preparation in the first semester, not the last. ₹14.20 LPA average for the 2023–25 batch.

Sustainability as a thread

ESG and responsible business run through every specialization, led by the group's Sustainability Director.
