The Format

Built around the fact
that you have a job

Modules are scheduled so that no one has to choose between a board meeting and a lecture.

Flexible modules

Content is delivered in self-contained modules rather than a fixed semester lockstep, so you can sequence around your work calendar.

Weekend-weighted sessions

Core sessions sit on weekends, with intensives scheduled well in advance so travel and client commitments can be planned around them.

Case-based, not lecture-based

Immersive case sessions where the cohort's own operating experience is part of the material. You will be asked to defend decisions you have actually made.

Industry-led faculty

Taught by practitioners — CMOs, CHROs, chartered accountants and digital officers who are still doing the work they teach.

Personalised learning

Small cohorts mean the faculty know your context. Assignments can be anchored to problems inside your own organisation.

ASMi certification

Completion carries certification from ASMi Business School, part of Techno India University, West Bengal.
