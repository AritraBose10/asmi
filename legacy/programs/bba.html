The Idea

An undergraduate degree
that graduates job-ready

Most BBAs teach management theory and hope analytics arrives later. We inverted it: the analytical toolkit comes first, and management practice is built on top of it.

Analytics from semester one

Spreadsheets, statistics, SQL and visualisation start immediately — not as an elective in the final year.

IBM tooling in the lab

Hands-on work with IBM-powered analytics content, plus Google Cloud skilling pathways through the Techno India Group partnership.

Live projects every year

Each year carries a real brief from a real organisation. By graduation you have a portfolio, not just a transcript.

A degree that stacks

Graduates move directly into the ASMi MBA with a year of the analytics core already behind them.

The Four Years

Year by year,
skill by skill

Each year closes with a live project, so progress is measured in work produced rather than papers passed.

01Year

Foundations

Business fundamentals and the analytical base — accounting, economics, statistics and the first data tooling.

  • Principles of Management
  • Financial Accounting
  • Business Statistics
  • Spreadsheet & Data Fundamentals
  • Year-one live brief
02Year

Functions and data

The functional areas arrive alongside databases and visualisation — you analyse the functions as you learn them.

  • Marketing & Consumer Behaviour
  • Corporate Finance Basics
  • Databases & SQL
  • Data Visualisation
  • Business Research Methods
03Year

Analytics in practice

The specialization year: predictive analytics, business intelligence and an internship that puts the toolkit to work.

  • Predictive Analytics
  • Business Intelligence with IBM
  • Operations & Supply Chain Analytics
  • Summer internship
  • Club-led datathon
04Year

Strategy and the capstone

Strategic thinking, ethics and a capstone analytics project defended before industry practitioners.

  • Strategic Management
  • Business Ethics & ESG
  • Capstone analytics project
  • Placement & MBA pathway preparation
