Life on the 15th floor

ASMi sits inside Godrej Genesis in Sector V — Kolkata's technology district. You study surrounded by the companies you are preparing to work for, which changes the texture of an ordinary Tuesday.

The Campus

A vertical campus in the
middle of the working city

Not a sprawling suburban campus you have to leave to find industry. The industry is in the building next door.

Business library

Print and digital business collections, case libraries and quiet study space for exam season.

Case rooms

Tiered discussion rooms built for the case method — every seat faces the argument.

Analytics lab

Workstations for the IBM-powered analytics modules, open outside class hours for club clinics.

Smart classrooms

Connected teaching spaces for hybrid sessions, guest lectures and remote industry panels.

Student lounge

Where club meetings, case prep and most of the friendships actually happen.

Sector V on your doorstep

IT parks, corporate offices and the recruiters you will meet — all within walking distance.

A Week Here

What an ordinary
week contains

MonDay

Core sessions and case prep

The teaching week opens with core modules. Case groups form in the lounge by late afternoon.

  • Core lectures
  • Case group formation
  • Library hours
WedDay

Guest lecture or corporate connect

A practitioner takes the room — a live problem from their own desk, fortnightly.

  • Guest lecture series
  • Corporate connect sessions
  • Q&A with hiring managers
FriDay

Club activity

The clubs own Friday: datathon heats, stock pitches, brand audits, rehearsals.

  • Club clinics and competitions
  • Council meetings
  • Open mic and cultural practice
SatDay

Workshops and field work

Skill workshops, placement preparation, and the field visits that anchor coursework to real operations.

  • Placement workshops
  • Field and company visits
  • Community projects
