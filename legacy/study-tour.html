The Programme

What the ten days
actually contain

Not a sightseeing trip with a lecture attached. The itinerary is built around how businesses in another market actually operate.

Sessions at INTI International University

Engagement with industry experts and global academics on the INTI campus — the academic spine of the tour.

Royal Selangor pewter factory

A working manufacturing floor: craft production, quality systems and how a heritage brand scales.

Barleys Chocolate factory

Food manufacturing end to end — supply chain, process control and brand storytelling in one visit.

Cultural heritage fieldwork

Structured visits to heritage sites, reading a market through its culture rather than its spreadsheets.

Why It Matters

You cannot read a market
from a case study

Business practice differs across borders

Negotiation norms, supply chain structures, consumer expectations and regulatory posture all shift the moment you cross a border. Seeing that first-hand is the fastest way to stop assuming your home market is the default.

A cohort travels differently than a class sits

Ten days in an unfamiliar country does something to a group that two years of shared classrooms does not. Most alumni describe the tour as the point the batch became a network.
