The Process

How the placement cell works

Placement support is a two-year process, not a two-week scramble at the end.

01Stage

Profile building

From the first semester: CV construction, LinkedIn presence and a clear read on which roles actually fit you.

  • CV and profile clinics
  • LinkedIn and personal branding with our CHRO faculty
  • Role and sector mapping
02Stage

Skill preparation

Aptitude, case method, domain revision and the analytics tooling recruiters test for.

  • Case interview practice
  • Aptitude and domain drills
  • IBM-powered analytics practice
03Stage

Industry exposure

Corporate connect sessions, live projects and the summer internship that often converts.

  • Monthly corporate connect
  • Summer internship
  • Live industry projects
04Stage

Placement season

Mock panels with practising managers, then the recruitment process itself.

  • Mock interview panels
  • Recruiter engagement
  • Offer negotiation guidance
