How We Teach

What our faculty actually do differently

Interactive and participatory

Sessions are built around discussion and defence of decisions rather than transmission of notes.

Industry-anchored

Faculty hold or have held senior positions — the examples come from their own desks.

Research-active

Faculty conduct research and publish, keeping the classroom connected to current thinking.

Individually mentored

Small cohorts mean faculty mentor individual students through specialisation and placement.
