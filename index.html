ASMi Business School is a prestigious institution affiliated with Techno India University, West Bengal. "ASMI" is Sanskrit for "I am" — symbolizing self-belief and individual potential. We offer AICTE-approved MBA, BBA, and Executive MBA programs from our campus at Godrej Genesis, Sector V, Kolkata.

We offer MBA (2 years, 6 specializations), BBA in Business Analytics (4 years), Executive MBA for working professionals, and 3-month certification courses in Marketing 4.0, HR Excellence, and Entrepreneurship 4.0 — all in partnership with IBM.

Yes. Our programs are AICTE-approved and affiliated with Techno India University, a recognized and accredited institution. We are also partnered with IBM and Google Cloud for curriculum development.
